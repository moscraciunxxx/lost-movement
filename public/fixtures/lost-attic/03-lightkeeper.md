# The Lightkeeper

There is no inland lighthouse. Forget the inland lighthouse. I keep the real one, and it stands in salt, not wheat. The beam does not care about your bars.

They sent me a note about the house pitch of A440. The house never tunes to A440. We keep A432 because the foghorn already occupies the beating that 440 would make against the generator. I have written this on the door. They paint over the door.

The silver metronome at dusk is a lie they tell visitors. Nothing silver survives the spray. What they saw was a tin can swinging on a string.

I do hear, some nights, a chamber of unplayed notes — the wind in the lantern gallery making a stack of empty fifths. That is the only orchestra I trust. Come out here. Stop inventing chapels in fields.
