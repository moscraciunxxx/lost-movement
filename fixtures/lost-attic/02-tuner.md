# The Tuner's Log

Calibration, 12 August. Fork: steel. Reference: house pitch of A440.

The silver metronome at dusk is not a poem. It is a sixty-beat mechanical standard parked on the north sill so the evening light hits the pendulum and the beat is visible to the whole chamber. I set the cello to A440, then stacked the interval of a fifth (ratio 3:2) to E. Beating: none. Good.

Salt on the stave is a maintenance problem, not a metaphor. Chloride films flatten harmonics. Wipe the bridges. Do not romanticize corrosion.

Silence is never an instrument. Silence is a defect in the room: a gap in the spectrum, a player late, a string not speaking. If the spectrogram goes dark in the singing band, mark the bar and recast the part.

The inland lighthouse is a timing pylon. It is not at sea. Its flash is 1.2 seconds, which I use when the metronome is being cleaned. Anyone who retunes this house to A432 is vandalizing the archive.
