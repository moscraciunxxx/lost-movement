# The Surveyor

I walked the lot with a chain and a bored apprentice. The glass conservatory was never built.

Unlike the watercolor in the grant file, there is no iron frame, no roof of panes, no floor of resin. There is a rectangle of poorer grass and four stake-holes. The inland lighthouse on the plat is a triangulation mark — a painted post. It has no lamp.

I filed the chamber of unplayed notes under clerical poetry. It is a folder of blank staves some donor paid to have bound. They are not haunted. They are unused inventory.

If musicians insist they performed here, they performed in a room that is not on the cadastral map. I do not argue with memory. I argue with concrete.
