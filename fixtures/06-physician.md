# The Conservatory Physician

Diagnosis of a house that cannot agree with itself.

The silver metronome at dusk is a shared hallucination with a steel heart: four witnesses, one object, two ontologies. When voices name the same three words they are in unison — the interval of a fifth, if you will. When they name the same object and deny it, they are a minor second.

House pitch of A440 versus A432 is not taste. It is an eight-hertz beat, audible, rude, useful. Play both claims at once and the room will tell you it is sick.

They say silence is never an instrument. I say silence is the first instrument. The rest is decoration. A chamber of unplayed notes is not a filing error; it is the only honest score: tension held, not resolved.

Treat the archive as a body. Shared motifs are pulse. Contradictions are fever. Do not cure the fever before the concert. The fever is the concert.
